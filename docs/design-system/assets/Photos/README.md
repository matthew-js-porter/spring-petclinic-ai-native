# Photos

- `pets.png`: the author's own photo of their dog lying in a fluffy cream bed, hugging a stuffed toy dog (720×960, metadata stripped). The app serves it from `static/resources/images/pets.png`.

Use it only on the homepage, under the "Welcome" title, as `.pc-hero`: `hero-width` (360px) wide and never wider than its container, square, no border, shadow or card. Its `alt` is "A small grey and white long-haired dog lying on its back in a fluffy cream bed, hugging a brown and white stuffed toy dog". This is the system's only photograph; see Iconography in the main README.
